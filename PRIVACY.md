# ChatGraph Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

ChatGraph is a browser extension that displays ChatGPT conversation branches and helps you navigate between conversations and messages.

## Local data processing

ChatGraph reads conversation titles, identifiers, messages, message versions, and branch relationships from chatgpt.com. It stores conversation data, branch relationships, history scan progress, and language preferences in your browser's local extension storage. Temporary tab and navigation state is kept in browser session storage.

The developer does not receive or collect this data. ChatGraph does not upload conversation data to developer servers or other external services, sell data, use data for advertising or credit decisions, or include analytics or tracking services.

## ChatGPT access

ChatGraph communicates with chatgpt.com to retrieve your conversations and history using your existing signed-in session. It temporarily reads the session access token to authenticate these requests to ChatGPT. The token is not saved in extension storage or transmitted to the developer. ChatGraph does not ask for or store your password.

Access to page content and navigation is limited to chatgpt.com. ChatGraph does not collect browsing history from other websites. Conversation content may contain personal or sensitive information you have entered into ChatGPT; ChatGraph processes that content locally only to provide its conversation tree, previews, and navigation features.

Your use of ChatGPT remains subject to OpenAI's privacy policy and terms. Local processing by ChatGraph does not change how ChatGPT handles data on its own service.

## Retention and deletion

Saved conversation data remains in local extension storage until that storage is cleared or the extension is uninstalled. Uninstalling ChatGraph removes its browser extension storage. This does not delete conversations from your ChatGPT account. Temporary session storage is cleared when the browser session ends.
